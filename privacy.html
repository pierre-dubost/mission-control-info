Privacy policy

This policy describes Dashboard, called Mission Control inside Hermes Desktop, a personal-use application operated by Pierre Dubost. It also describes this public information website.

1. Information accessed

  • Google account identity: the connected email address, used to verify and separate accounts.
  • Gmail: selected conversation and message identifiers, sender and recipient headers, subject lines, timestamps, message text, source labels and attachment metadata. Attachment files are not downloaded by the Google collector.
  • Google Calendar: calendar identifiers and list metadata, plus event details from explicitly selected calendars. Details may include titles, descriptions, locations, dates, attendees and response status.
  • Optional Discord: text and metadata from approved conversations the integration can access. Unread attachments and other non-text content are disclosed as context limitations.
  • Local application records: derived findings and source excerpts, user confirmations, saved tasks, Done/Snooze/Pin/Dismiss choices, optional action notes, history, settings and diagnostic metadata.
  • Authorization information: OAuth access and refresh credentials needed to maintain the approved Google connection.

Google access is limited to Gmail read-only, Calendar events read-only and Calendar list read-only permissions. The application’s selection settings further restrict which sources are retrieved.

2. Purpose and use

Information is used to create a private, source-linked briefing, identify potential user-owned follow-ups, show upcoming events, retain saved decisions, avoid repeating valid work and explain incomplete updates. It is not used to sell data, serve advertisements or build advertising profiles.

The Google integration does not send or modify email, change read/unread state, edit calendar events or answer invitations. Local handling actions do not change the underlying Google or Discord records.

3. AI processing and sharing

Selected Gmail or Discord conversation text, associated metadata, review policy and relevant calibration information are transmitted to the AI service configured by the operator in Hermes. The service returns candidate findings that the application validates against the source. Not every account item is sent on every refresh.

This means processing is not entirely local. The configured AI provider and its retention and data-use terms can vary with the operator’s configuration. The operator must use a provider configuration appropriate to the connected data and the permissions of anyone whose information it contains. The application has no model-training pipeline.

Authorization credentials, optional completion notes and local decision history are not included in AI review prompts. Account data is not published on this website or in its repository. No independent human-review service is part of the application.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Transfers are limited to providing the user-facing features described here, user-authorized operations, security purposes or applicable legal requirements.

4. Storage and protection

Collected source copies, review caches, derived findings, settings and local decisions are stored in the operator’s local Hermes environment. Credentials are kept in a separate protected account store. Local filesystem access controls restrict access; this is not a claim that all local files are encrypted.

Google and AI connections use HTTPS. If the operator enables the separate backup facility, selected local settings and task records may also be included in encrypted backups stored at the operator’s chosen destination. Credentials are excluded from that backup inventory.

5. Retention and removal

Source copies and review caches may remain locally until replaced or removed. Saved tasks, notes and decision history persist independently of refresh results; Dismiss hides an item and is not deletion. There is no promise of automatic deletion after a fixed number of days.

The operator can stop using the integration and revoke Google access in the Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revocation prevents future authorized retrieval but does not erase copies already stored locally, in backups or by an external AI service.

For removal, the operator must delete the relevant local application records and any retained backup copies. Provider-held information is subject to the provider’s deletion controls and retention terms. Contact the operator below for questions or assistance concerning records handled by this application.

6. This public website

This is a static information site hosted with GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in, data-entry forms, advertising, embedded analytics, third-party fonts or application-data API. GitHub may process standard hosting request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The site does not access connected Gmail, Calendar or Discord accounts.

7. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the application’s data practices change. Material new uses or permissions require notice and any necessary consent before use.
